Before you share a photo, know — and remove — what it says about you

PhotoVeil is a planned local-first app, iOS first with Android to follow from the same codebase. It is not built yet. This page exists to answer one question first: do enough people want batch photo-metadata cleaning that runs entirely on their own phone — no upload, no account, no tracking — to justify building it and paying the app-store fees? If yes, it gets built. If not, it doesn't.

Why this shape

  • Photos routinely carry GPS coordinates, device model, and timestamps. Online "EXIF remover" websites ask you to upload the very photos you are trying to protect — an on-device tool removes that contradiction.
  • The local-first, one-time-purchase model has worked for independent apps in adjacent categories: developers of single-purpose local apps have publicly reported first-year sales in the low thousands of copies at $3.99–$9.99 price points (developer self-reports, 2019–2020). That proves the model can exist, not that this app will sell.
  • A concrete everyday case: sending a photo of an ID card, a contract, or a payslip to a landlord, a platform, or a stranger. A purpose watermark ("for XX verification only") plus stripped metadata is a small, specific protection people currently improvise with markup tools.

Planned capabilities (none of this exists yet)

  • Batch-remove EXIF/GPS/device metadata from selected photos, entirely on-device — no upload, no server, no analytics
  • A "what this photo reveals" view before cleaning: location, device, time, software
  • Optional purpose watermark for ID or sensitive photos before sharing
  • Batch HEIC → JPEG/WebP conversion
  • Free tier (planned): single-photo cleaning. Lifetime unlock (planned): batch processing, watermarking, conversion

This page promises nothing that is not written above. Features, if the product is built, will follow what registrants actually ask for.

Who this is for

  • People who post or send photos and do not want their home location embedded in them
  • Second-hand sellers, photographers, and anyone who shares ID or document photos
  • If you never share photos, or your platform already strips everything to your satisfaction, you do not need this

Questions

Can I use it now?
No. Nothing has been built; this page is the whole thing.

Does registering mean I have to buy it?
No. It only means "notify me if it launches". Decide when there is a real product to look at.

Why iOS first, and why the wait?
An iOS developer account costs $99/year and Android adds its own registration and testing period. Spending that before anyone has asked for the app would be backwards; registrations come first, fees second.

Why not just use a free website?
You can — but the photo has to be uploaded to someone else's server first, which is exactly the exposure this idea exists to avoid.
